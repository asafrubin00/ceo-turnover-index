About the Index

Explore CEO appointments, departures and tenure across major stock indices and industries. The dashboard uses the quarterly figures published by Russell Reynolds Associates.

Turnover rates use the source’s published percentages. The company count is reported separately in the appendix. Benchmarks can overlap, so the trend chart plots each benchmark individually.
